
Seaching can be accessed through the dropdownmenu,
or through a key combination.

When searching, following search options should be possible:

Searching by text takes the given text and puts it into a regular
expression, then searches each card by its name in cardinfo.
Cards that are in the full cardlist, but not in the current group
will also be shown, but in a special state indicating it is not
inside the current group.

You can engulf the whole search in " quotes to
make the whole term treated as one search string.

If -I or -i or -intern or -Intern is leading, no cards outside
the current group will be shown in the search.
To instead search with this term, use "\" leading
in front of the term.
